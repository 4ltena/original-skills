# Japanese lexical cues

Read this after the syntax and flow pass. These are prompts to inspect meaning and audience, never words to ban or a claim about who wrote the text. A match alone does not warrant a change. Do not replace terms inside quotations, code, names or established project vocabulary.

For an explicit broad review of AI-like Japanese wording, or when polishing freelancer self-PR, internal messages with stiff greeting formulas, or prose with repeated slogan-like conclusions, read the optional [X-reported and user-supplied cues](x-reported-cues.md). Its sourced reports and user examples are separate evidence tiers. Do not load that larger dictionary for every routine polish.

| Cue | Inspect when | Keep when | Possible revision, only if the intended meaning is known | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 正本 | It loosely labels a project's repository or version without stating the relationship, or implies authority the supplied facts do not establish. | It denotes an actual legally effective copy, is contrasted with 副本, or is a defined project term. | State only the supported relationship. If the fact is merely that seven Skills are present, write “このリポジトリには文書 Skill が7件あります”. If maintenance is established, say which Skill or version is maintained there. Do not automatically substitute 原本, which can mean something different. | User-observed wording; [X criticism](https://x.com/_6v_/status/2096064539821506609), [X reading critique](https://x.com/yoko_bsf14/status/2099414536193458524), [X report of adoption](https://x.com/yukimi_inu/status/2099197591112364268), and [X defense of a technical meaning](https://x.com/ttdomot/status/2100126437135323385); [Japan Patent Office legal usage](https://www.jpo.go.jp/system/trial_appeal/shubetu-tokkyo-igi/document/syoko_setsumeisyo/03.pdf). No measured AI-specific frequency. |
| 包括的 / 網羅的 / 多角的 | A broad claim of coverage has no visible scope or basis, especially when several such adjectives accumulate. | The scope, dimensions or coverage criterion is defined and supported. | Name the covered items or dimensions if the source supplies them; otherwise qualify or remove the unsupported evaluation. Do not invent a list. | [Practitioner observation](https://qiita.com/Mildsolt2914491/items/64f17be4ab8a9a562853), not a general frequency estimate. |
| 活用 / 踏まえて | Repetition hides the concrete action or relation. | It precisely describes using a resource or considering a stated premise in this context. | Say what is used, how, or which premise informs which decision, if the source makes that clear. A simple “use” may suffice for an ordinary action. | [Practitioner observation](https://qiita.com/Mildsolt2914491/items/64f17be4ab8a9a562853); context determines suitability. |
| 当該 | In a general-audience document, it points back to an unclear item or gives a routine instruction an unnecessarily administrative register. | A legal or technical context needs its precise reference, or the term is established for that document. | If only one referent is possible, name it or use その with the noun. If several are possible, resolve the reference from the source or flag it. | [Agency guidance on matching register to readers](https://www.bunka.go.jp/koho_hodo_oshirase/hodohappyo/93650001.html); a [historical survey](https://www.bunka.go.jp/tokei_hakusho_shuppan/hakusho_nenjihokokusho/archive/pdf/94052201_01.pdf) records a proposal to use その. Neither establishes AI-specific overuse. |
| 静かに / 余白 / 扉 | A practical report or notice uses a mood or grand metaphor where no literal condition or intended image is relevant. | Quietness or space is literal, or the image is deliberate and fits the author's voice and medium. | Remove only an expendable flourish, or state a documented change instead. Do not invent emotion, impact or events. | [Writer's subjective examples of 静かに and 扉](https://note.com/y_maguchi/n/ne36d0cf542be) and [余白](https://note.com/xenotro/n/n1e5c3a1f1af6); [X criticism of 静かに](https://x.com/ura_urara_money/status/2093634242215375138) and [X praise for 余白](https://x.com/daruo_nemunemu/status/2100550080638119966). No frequency or authorship inference. |
| 伴走する / 寄り添う / 無理なく / 一歩ずつ | A promise of help or ease leaves the provider, action, recipient or conditions unstated. | The service or relationship is explained and the promise is supported, or the phrasing deliberately serves an appropriate empathetic voice. | If the source specifies support, name that action and its limits. Otherwise retain a material promise and flag the missing details; remove it only if it adds no distinct claim. Do not invent a service or swap in an equally vague promise such as サポートする. | [Writer's subjective examples](https://note.com/xenotro/n/n1e5c3a1f1af6); no prevalence claim. |
| 本質 / 解像度 / 言語化 | An abstract label substitutes for the actual distinction, finding or thing expressed. | It is a defined concept, a useful field term or a concise label for a finding the text already explains. | State the identified issue or distinction when the source supplies it. If a material claim remains unclear, retain and flag it instead of inventing an explanatory benefit. | [Writer's subjective examples](https://note.com/xenotro/n/n1e5c3a1f1af6); meaning depends on context. |
| といえるでしょう / ではないでしょうか / そう思いませんか | A formulaic hedge obscures the claim's supported certainty, or a question asks for agreement without a real reader-facing purpose. | The uncertainty matters, the question genuinely invites a decision or response, or it is part of the author's deliberate voice. | Match the statement to its actual evidence; omit an empty question if it adds no information. Never make an uncertain claim categorical. | [Writer's subjective examples](https://note.com/y_maguchi/n/ne36d0cf542be); a [limited corpus comparison](https://qiita.com/suwa_nobu/items/4231026d13f7527c0f69) found no clear frequency difference for selected phrases. |

For each candidate, identify the intended sense, reader cost, protected use and smallest meaning-preserving alternative. If any is unknown, leave the word and flag the ambiguity. Do not compute an “AI score” or run a global substitution. Add future cues only with an attested example, a valid-use exception, a possible context-sensitive rewrite and provenance.
