# Limited review of a completed novel

Inspect and edit only these three categories. Ground findings in the scene and propose the smallest alternative.

1. **Formulaic vocabulary:** Evaluative or abstract words that clarify nothing about the scene's people, actions or senses. Keep frequent words when they perform a concrete scene function.
2. **Unnatural connectives:** Connections that misstate the relationship between events or mechanically interrupt scene flow. Frequency alone is insufficient; retain a character's distinctive language.
3. **Mechanical ending repetition:** Nearby endings that become unintentionally monotonous. Preserve deliberate repetition, dialogue habits and rhythms that create tension or aftereffects.

Do not inspect web-article openings/conclusions, lists, dashes, three-part lists or uniform politeness in this mode. Add no first-person reactions, new experiences, descriptions or metaphors. Preserve viewpoint, narrative distance and scene pace.
